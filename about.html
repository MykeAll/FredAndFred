OUR CORE FOCUS

On the average, the agricultural Industry in Nigeria and West Africa is still largely on a peasant level where farmers uses hand-held equipment for agricultural production.

Fred & Fred’s main focus is to create a sustainable environment by the way of transferring know-how in manufacturing to support the direct value chain required to mechanize our agricultural industry in Nigeria and the entire West African sub-region.

We have network of competent resources to deploy on a world wide basis to support the take-off of our initiatives.
