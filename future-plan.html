LOCALIZATION CASE STUDY

THE ALGERIAN EXAMPLE

01

YOU BENEFIT

When the combine is shipped in pre-assemblies, the shipping costs are remarkably low. You can benefit from local manpower and start your own assembly business and sales program. In certain countries the state may provide financial support when assembly production is started. The state may also support the final customer when purchasing a local product. In co-operation with Sampo Rosenlew the local manufacturer can start his own production operations and, as the knowhow increases, start his own after sales business. Local manufacturing enables our partners to use local suppliers, manpower and financing. When the factory is located in the business area, customers may visit the factory. This is crucial when building up long-term, satisfied customer relationships with the end-users.

02

YOUR BUSINESS EXPANDS

As you become more familiar with the product and the manufacturing process, you can diversify your business into part manufacturing and after sales functions such as spare parts and customer support. As you establish a reputation with your brand and product line, you will soon have your own sales and customer network and become more and more independent. With our network and connections supporting your sales and business, there are great opportunities for successful co-operation and combine harvester manufacturing.
